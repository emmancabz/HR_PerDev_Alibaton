## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes minimum organizational expectations for recognizing workplace hazards, reporting unsafe conditions, escalating safety concerns, and documenting corrective action.

## 2. Scope
Applies to employees, trainees, supervisors, and personnel working in operational areas, project sites, yards, logistics areas, offices, and other company work locations.

## 3. Policy Statements
- Safety concerns must be reported promptly through the approved reporting channel.
- Employees and trainees must not ignore an unsafe condition because it appears minor or temporary.
- Only trained and authorized personnel may perform specialized or safety-sensitive work.
- Personnel must follow approved site controls, posted notices, manufacturer guidance, and supervisor instructions applicable to their role.
- Work must be paused and escalated when continuing may create an immediate safety risk.
- Safety reports must be factual and identify the observed condition, location, date/time, and persons notified where applicable.
- Retaliation for raising a good-faith safety concern is not acceptable.
- Corrective actions must be assigned, tracked, and verified before closure.

## 4. Responsibilities
Employees and trainees recognize hazards, follow controls, report unsafe conditions, and cooperate with corrective action. Supervisors review concerns, apply controls within authority, escalate unresolved risks, and reinforce required behavior. Safety & Compliance maintains guidance and tracks corrective actions. HR/Training coordinates learning or refresher interventions where recurring gaps are identified.

## 5. Required Records
Applicable records may include hazard reports, incident reports, inspection findings, corrective-action logs, attendance records, and learning/training completion evidence.

## 6. Learning Use
Supports LMS content on hazard recognition, incident reporting, safety inspection, corrective action, and permit/compliance awareness.
