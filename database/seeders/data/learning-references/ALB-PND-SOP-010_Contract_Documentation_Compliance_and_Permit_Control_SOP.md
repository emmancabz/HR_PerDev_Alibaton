## 1. Purpose
Defines the minimum process for maintaining contract documents, tracking obligations, controlling changes, retaining supporting correspondence, and monitoring permit/regulatory documentation.

## 2. Contract File Requirements
Contract records should include the current approved agreement, relevant schedules, amendments/change records, approvals, correspondence, submissions, and supporting evidence.

## 3. Change and Compliance Control
Material changes must be documented and approved. Obligations and deadlines must be tracked. Supporting evidence must be retained for significant decisions, changes, claims, or compliance actions. Unresolved compliance concerns must be escalated.

## 4. Permit and Regulatory Documentation
Required permits or regulatory records must be identified, tracked for validity where applicable, and stored in a controlled location. Expired, superseded, or incomplete records must not be treated as current authorization.

## 5. Learning Use
Supports contract documentation, compliance monitoring, change/claims fundamentals, permit documentation, and records control.

## Document Control
Any material revision must update the version and trigger review of affected LMS course versions, assessments, completion rules, or learner assignments where applicable.
