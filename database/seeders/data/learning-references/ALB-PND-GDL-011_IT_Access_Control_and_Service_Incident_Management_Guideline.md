## 1. Purpose
Establishes high-level expectations for managing user access and handling IT service or security-related incidents.

## 2. Access Control
- Access is based on authorized role and business need.
- Account creation, role changes, elevated access, suspension, and revocation must be traceable.
- Shared personal accounts are not permitted.
- Elevated or administrative access is limited to authorized personnel.
- Access should be reviewed when responsibilities or employment status change.

## 3. Incident Management
IT issues are recorded through the approved support process, categorized, assigned, tracked, and closed with documented resolution. Security-related events are escalated through the security process. Relevant records must be preserved.

## 4. Learning Use
Supports service-desk operations, systems administration, access governance, technical security awareness, escalation, and auditability.
