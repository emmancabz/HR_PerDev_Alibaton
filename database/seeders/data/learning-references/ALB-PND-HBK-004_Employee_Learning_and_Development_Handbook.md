## 1. Purpose
The Learning and Development program supports employees and trainees in maintaining role competence, addressing development gaps, preparing for changing responsibilities, and building capability for future organizational needs.

## 2. Sources of Development Needs
Development may originate from onboarding requirements, role requirements, performance reviews, competency gaps, coaching discussions, safety/compliance findings, succession development plans, employee requests, or new processes and systems.

## 3. Development Methods
Development may include LMS courses, facilitated training, supervised practice, coaching, mentoring, stretch assignments, refresher learning, or other approved activities.

## 4. Responsibilities
Learners complete assigned development honestly and on time. Supervisors discuss needs and support workplace application. HR/Training governs the catalog, records, assignments, and completion. Subject Matter Experts validate technical accuracy.

## 5. Completion and Evidence
Learning completion must follow approved course rules. Completion records, assessment results, transcripts, and certificates must remain traceable to the learner and course version.

## 6. Career Progression
Completion of learning or training does not guarantee promotion, appointment, or a specific performance rating. Learning evidence may support competency development and succession assessment together with other finalized evidence.

## Document Control
Any material revision must update the version and trigger review of affected LMS course versions, assessments, completion rules, or learner assignments where applicable.
