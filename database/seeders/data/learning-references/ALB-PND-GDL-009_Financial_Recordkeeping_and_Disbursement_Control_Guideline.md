## 1. Purpose
Supports consistent financial recordkeeping, review, reconciliation, approval, and reporting practices.

## 2. Core Controls
- Financial entries must be supported by appropriate source records.
- Required approvals must be obtained before controlled transactions are released.
- Preparation, review, approval, and payment responsibilities should be separated where applicable.
- Exceptions, missing documents, unusual amounts, and reconciliation differences must be investigated and documented.
- Budget and cost variances must be supported by traceable evidence and explanatory notes.
- Financial records must be retained in approved locations.
- Confidential financial information is accessible only to authorized personnel.

## 3. Learning Use
Supports financial recordkeeping, accounts payable/disbursement controls, budget monitoring, cost control, audit trail, and exception handling.
