## Training Purpose
Defines the learning focus for personnel involved in crane operations, rigging support, signaling, coordination, inspection, or maintenance.

## Learning Focus
Participants are expected to understand role boundaries and authorization; pre-work communication; conditions requiring stop-work and escalation; approved communication practices; equipment/site/regulatory requirements; inspection and defect-reporting responsibilities; and accurate training/maintenance records.

## Practical Training
Where practical evaluation is required, it must be conducted only by an authorized facilitator in an approved environment using the applicable safety procedures. LMS completion alone must not be represented as authorization to perform specialized work.

## Assessment
Knowledge assessments may be delivered through the LMS. Practical competency must be recorded separately in Training Management when required.

## Certificate
A course or training certificate confirms completion of the approved requirement; it does not replace any external license, statutory certification, or authorization required for specialized work.

Any material revision must update the version and trigger review of affected LMS course versions, assessments, completion rules, or learner assignments where applicable.
