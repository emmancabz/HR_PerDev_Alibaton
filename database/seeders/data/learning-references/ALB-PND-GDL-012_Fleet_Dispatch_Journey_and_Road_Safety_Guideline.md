## 1. Purpose
Supports safe and coordinated dispatch, journey planning, scheduling, transport documentation, and delivery handover.

## 2. Requirements
- Dispatch assignments identify responsible personnel, schedule, destination, and relevant constraints.
- Personnel comply with applicable road laws, company requirements, authorized routes, and vehicle requirements.
- Unsafe, fatigued, or otherwise unfit conditions are reported and escalated.
- Schedule pressure must not justify unsafe conduct.
- Delivery changes, delays, incidents, and significant exceptions are recorded and communicated.
- Load-related requirements follow approved transport procedures and role authorization.
- Handover records must be complete enough for the next responsible person to understand status and open actions.

## 3. Learning Use
Supports fleet dispatch, scheduling, defensive-driving awareness, transport coordination, exception handling, and operational handover.
