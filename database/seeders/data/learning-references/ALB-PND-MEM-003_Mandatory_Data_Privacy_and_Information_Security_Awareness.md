## Subject
Mandatory Data Privacy and Information Security Awareness

All personnel are expected to protect company, employee, client, project, and operational information accessed through approved devices, applications, records, and communication channels.

## Required Practices
- Use only authorized accounts and approved systems for company work.
- Keep passwords, authentication codes, recovery information, and access credentials confidential.
- Do not share an account with another person.
- Verify unusual requests for confidential information or account access.
- Report suspected phishing, unauthorized access, lost devices, or accidental disclosure through the approved escalation channel.
- Access only information needed for assigned work.
- Do not copy, export, forward, or store confidential information in an unapproved location.
- Lock or sign out of shared or unattended workstations.
- Follow retention, archive, and deletion rules applicable to company records.

## Learning Requirement
The organization may assign recurring privacy and security awareness courses and additional technical security learning for personnel with elevated or technical access.
