## 1. Purpose
Defines the information that must be transferred when responsibility for work, records, tasks, equipment coordination, or pending actions moves from one employee, shift, or team to another.

## 2. Required Handover Content
A handover includes, when applicable: current work status; completed actions; pending tasks and responsible persons; unresolved issues, risks, or constraints; deadlines or scheduled activities; required records/references; pending approvals; and safety/compliance matters needing attention.

## 3. Handover Process
1. Prepare an accurate status summary using the approved channel.
2. Clearly identify priority items, deadlines, risks, and unresolved matters.
3. The receiving person reviews and requests clarification where needed.
4. Ownership of open actions is confirmed.
5. Required records are updated for traceability.
6. Significant unresolved risks are escalated to the appropriate supervisor.

## 4. Quality Requirements
Handovers must be concise, factual, current, and understandable. Assumptions must not be presented as verified facts.

## 5. Learning Use
Supports learning on operational communication, work turnover, records continuity, escalation, administrative coordination, and project-site coordination.
