## 1. Purpose
Establishes common expectations for creating, classifying, storing, retrieving, updating, retaining, and disposing of organizational records.

## 2. Requirements
- Records must be stored in approved locations or systems.
- Official records must be identifiable, current, and attributable to the responsible function.
- Material changes to controlled documents must be versioned or traceable.
- Superseded documents must not be presented as current guidance.
- Access is limited according to role, confidentiality, and business need.
- Required approvals and supporting evidence must be retained.
- Retention and disposal must follow organizational, contractual, and legal requirements.
- Missing, inconsistent, or unauthorized document changes must be escalated.

## 3. Learning Use
Supports courses on records management, contract documentation, permit documentation, HR data handling, and audit trail.

## Document Control
Any material revision must update the version and trigger review of affected LMS course versions, assessments, completion rules, or learner assignments where applicable.
