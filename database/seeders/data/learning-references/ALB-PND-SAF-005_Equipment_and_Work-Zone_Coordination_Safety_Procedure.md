## 1. Purpose
Establishes high-level safety requirements for coordinating people, equipment, and controlled work areas.

## 2. General Requirements
- Specialized equipment may only be operated or serviced by trained and authorized personnel.
- Personnel must follow approved site procedures, manufacturer instructions, supervision requirements, and applicable regulations.
- Work areas must be organized to reduce uncontrolled interaction between moving equipment and people.
- Required communication methods and role responsibilities must be confirmed before safety-sensitive work begins.
- Personnel must remain outside restricted areas unless their role requires entry and authorization is present.
- Equipment or work must be stopped when unsafe conditions, unclear communication, unauthorized entry, or abnormal conditions are observed.
- Defects or abnormal equipment behavior must be reported and evaluated by authorized personnel before continued use.
- Maintenance, inspection, and return-to-service records must be maintained where required.

## 3. Training Requirement
Personnel assigned to safety-sensitive equipment, rigging, signaling, inspection, or maintenance responsibilities must complete applicable approved learning and practical training.

## 4. Learning Use
Supports high-level LMS instruction. It does not replace equipment-specific manuals, certified technical training, site-specific plans, or regulatory requirements.
