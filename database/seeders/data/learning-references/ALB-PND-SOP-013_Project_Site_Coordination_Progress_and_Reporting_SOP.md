## 1. Purpose
Defines common expectations for coordinating project-site activities, documenting work progress, identifying constraints, and reporting status.

## 2. Daily Coordination
Responsible personnel confirm priorities, planned activities, responsible persons, dependencies, constraints, required approvals, and issues requiring escalation.

## 3. Progress Evidence
Progress reporting must be based on verifiable work status or approved records. Estimates or assumptions must be clearly identified.

## 4. Variances and Constraints
Significant delay, resource constraint, quality concern, safety issue, or deviation from the approved plan must be recorded and escalated.

## 5. Handover and Reporting
Open actions, decisions, milestones, and constraints must be carried forward through an auditable handover or status-reporting process.

## 6. Learning Use
Supports project-site coordination, work planning, project controls, progress measurement, variance analysis, and management reporting.
