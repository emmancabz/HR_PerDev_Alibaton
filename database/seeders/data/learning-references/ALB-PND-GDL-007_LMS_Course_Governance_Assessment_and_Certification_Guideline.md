## 1. Purpose
Establishes minimum governance requirements for creating, reviewing, publishing, assessing, completing, and certifying LMS courses.

## 2. Course Governance
Each published course must have a course owner, target audience, objectives, controlled version, SME where needed, reviewer, authorized publisher, completion rules, assessment rules, and source/reference information where applicable.

## 3. Pre-Test
The Pre-Test is a required diagnostic assessment before course content begins. It establishes baseline knowledge, has no passing requirement, never blocks course access because of a low score, and retains the score for learning analysis.

## 4. Knowledge Checks
Knowledge checks may be placed within modules. The approved course version determines whether they are required for completion.

## 5. Post-Test
The Post-Test is the required course-level completion assessment unless an approved course version specifies another governed structure. Default passing score is 80%, with a maximum of three attempts. A failed attempt keeps the learner In Progress / Post-Test Retake.

## 6. Completion
Completed status requires all required lessons, required knowledge checks, and a passed Post-Test.

## 7. Certificates
Certificates may be issued only for configured courses. They must identify the learner, course, traceable course/version record, issue date, certificate number, and validity/expiry when applicable.

## 8. Historical Integrity
Published course versions and completed learning records remain traceable. Material changes create a new course version rather than rewriting historical completion evidence.

## Document Control
Any material revision must update the version and trigger review of affected LMS course versions, assessments, completion rules, or learner assignments where applicable.
